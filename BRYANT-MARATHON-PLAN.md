# Bryant comparison marathon plan

Race: January 31, 2027

This is a 16-week, six-day-per-week comparison plan built from a recent 30–34 mile baseline. Long runs are on Saturday except the Sunday marathon. Weekly mileage is:

`35, 38, 40, 34, 42, 45, 47, 39, 48, 50, 52, 44, 54, 48, 38, 40.2 (including the race)`

The lower-volume weeks are deliberate absorption weeks after three-week loading blocks. The actual taper begins after the January 16 long run.

## Pace tracks

- Stretch: 6:52/mi, approximately a 2:59:50 marathon
- Strong: 7:05/mi, approximately a 3:05:40 marathon
- Base: 7:20/mi, approximately a 3:12:10 marathon

These are marathon-specific test tracks, not labels for every run. Easy and recovery running should stay conversational even when faster running feels available.

## Decision gates

Use the December 12, December 26, and January 9 long runs to choose the race track. Move up only when the prescribed pace block is completed without a meaningful late fade, with stable form, controlled breathing, successful fueling, and an overall session RPE no higher than about 7/10. Hold the track when the session is completed but becomes strained. Move down or shorten the next quality session when pace deteriorates, form changes, a pain pattern develops, or recovery is still poor 48 hours later.

Short intervals are supporting evidence, not proof of marathon readiness. The January 9 simulation is the main race-target checkpoint.

## Using it in the app

Open Run and choose **Load Bryant plan** once. Use the View button to cycle through Both, Bryant, and Runna. Every workout can still be moved, swapped, edited, skipped, or deleted. After loading or editing the plan, use **Save to cloud**.
